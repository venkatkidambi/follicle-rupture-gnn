# Validation and experimental comparison — 2026-11-04

This is a working, MATLAB-checked differentiable inverse pipeline. The completed
synthetic experiments do **not** establish unique parameter recovery. The
experimental comparisons assess the initial fixed-topology model; they do not
establish discovery of mechanical connectivity or rupture sites.

## Synthetic evidence

The known network has nine noncoplanar nodes, 15 edges, three regional parameter
triples and a fixed two-edge susceptible patch. It includes contraction,
viscoelastic relaxation, damage, adhesion release and retraction. The first 19 of
25 saved times are used for fitting; the last six are held out. Units below are
the dimensionless synthetic model units.

| True/fitted family | Mean relative parameter error | Future vector RMSE | True / fitted sampled rupture time |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Elastic | 22.2% | 0.00548 | 1.125 / 1.125 |
| Maxwell | 29.9% | 0.00519 | 1.500 / 1.625 |
| Kelvin–Voigt | 31.0% | 0.00783 | 0.875 / 0.875 |
| Zener | 36.1% | 0.00728 | 1.000 / 1.000 |
| Burgers | 31.0% | 0.00427 | 1.750 / 1.750 |

Rupture means the first saved sample with `D >= 0.5`; saved times are 0.125
apart. Failed-edge intersection-over-union is 1.0 for all five cases, **conditional
on the supplied susceptibility mask**. This is a state-evolution check, not
recovery of an unknown damage location. Refining maximum integration step from
0.02 to 0.005 changes positions by at most `2.55e-5` model length units.

Parameter error includes the deliberately unidentifiable elastic relaxation
parameter. Other errors also reflect incomplete optimization and weak parameter
sensitivity; low trajectory error alone cannot distinguish those causes. The
tests demonstrate exact force/drag scale invariance, and an elastic element has
no viscosity dependence at all. Neither can be overcome by additional training.

On the **same known Zener observations**, fitted family forecast RMSE is:

| Candidate family | Future vector RMSE |
| --- | ---: |
| Elastic | 0.01321 |
| Maxwell | 0.02877 |
| Kelvin–Voigt | 0.01188 |
| Zener | 0.00728 |
| Burgers | 0.04293 |

This ranks the true family first for this one design and optimization budget. It
is not evidence that every pair of constitutive families can be distinguished
under arbitrary excitation or noise.

| Zener-data control | Clean future vector RMSE |
| --- | ---: |
| GNN, correct mechanics | 0.00620 |
| No damage | 0.03994 |
| Incorrect adhesion | 0.00761 |
| Incorrect topology (one omitted edge) | 0.03344 |
| Observation noise 0.002, seed 1 | 0.00750 |
| Observation noise 0.002, seed 2 | 0.00703 |

The GNN's normalized training loss falls from 0.37006 to 0.0007016. Removing
damage or the tested edge worsens prediction. The small error under incorrect
adhesion demonstrates why a good trajectory fit does not validate the assumed
boundary mechanics. Two noisy fits are a sensitivity control, not a statistical
coverage study or an exhaustive noise analysis.

The fitted synthetic Zener log-parameter Jacobian has numerical rank 9/9 at a
relative cutoff of `1e-5`, but singular values span 0.7029 to 0.00411 (condition
ratio approximately 171). Relaxation dominates the weakest direction. This is
local sensitivity under fixed graph, branch ratios and boundary assumptions;
full local rank does not establish global identifiability.

Full synthetic arrays and metrics: `results/gnn_synthetic/known_zener.npz` and
`results/gnn_synthetic/validation.json`.

## Experimental performance

Frames 0–23 (0–940.91 s) train the model; frames 24–34 (981.82–1,390.91 s)
are held out. There are 3,927 held-out node/frame samples, with no missing
observations in these retained frames. The coordinate transform is fixed from
frame zero. Length scale is 26.3472 µm; time scale is 300 s; node drag is fixed
to one. Reference masks include 827 active links, 22 susceptible links and 36
softly anchored nodes.

| Model | Future vector RMSE (µm) | Displacement magnitude MAE (µm) | Direction error (degrees) | Direction samples |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Stationary | 15.0156 | 12.8391 | undefined | 0 |
| Prefix linear motion | **8.9915** | **5.3964** | **25.42** | 3,927 |
| GNN elastic | 15.0177 | 12.7307 | 90.17 | 1,901 |
| GNN Maxwell | 15.0158 | 12.8320 | undefined | 0 |
| GNN Kelvin–Voigt | 15.0179 | 12.7236 | 90.66 | 2,070 |
| GNN Zener | 15.0179 | 12.7252 | 90.47 | 2,029 |
| GNN Burgers | 15.0158 | 12.8322 | undefined | 0 |
| Uniform Zener fit | 15.0763 | 11.7588 | 92.85 | 3,801 |
| Direct regional Zener fit | 15.0759 | 11.7291 | 92.45 | 3,812 |
| GNN Zener, no damage | 15.0179 | 12.7257 | 90.46 | 2,020 |

Direction requires both observed and simulated displacement above 0.1 µm.
Undefined values and reduced sample counts reflect nearly stationary predicted
motion, not correct direction. All metrics describe displacement from frame zero,
not a forecast reinitialized at the last training observation.

**The tested mechanical models do not beat the motion baselines.** GNN fits move
toward weak stiffness and contraction, and produce almost stationary forecasts.
They do not explain the measured direction of motion. This could reflect model,
boundary or initial-state misspecification as well as finite-budget optimization;
these experiments do not isolate the cause. No model family is validated by the
experimental comparison.

GNN Zener values are approximately `k1=k2=0.04157`, `tau=0.12506` and
`rate=0.015770` in all three regions, close to their lower bounds. Regional
stiffness differences are only about `3e-9` model units and have no demonstrated
physical significance. The map uses the full admissible stiffness color range
to avoid exaggerating these differences. Relaxation corresponds to about 37.5 s
under the chosen time convention, close to the 40.91 s acquisition interval;
this fitted number is not a reliable experimental measurement of relaxation.

### Conditional damage, rupture and uncertainty

All five GNN family fits predict zero damage and zero adhesion release in the
retained interval. The uniform Zener fit instead reaches `D >= 0.5` at 900 s,
with 13 failed links by the last frame; the direct regional fit first crosses at
940.91 s, with 12 failed links. Both are restricted to the prescribed tip mask
and both fit poorly. Their difference from the GNN shows that an inferred rupture
time depends strongly on the model/optimization assumptions. There is no
independent experimental rupture annotation in this dataset adapter. These
outputs are **not evidence that rupture was absent, present, or localized there**.

The GNN Zener training Jacobian has numerical rank 9/9, with singular values
0.1635 to 0.001382. Conditional log-parameter standard errors range from about
28 to 137, far beyond useful precision. Large residuals, boundary-hitting
coefficients and fixed-model assumptions make linearized Gaussian intervals
inappropriate as physical confidence claims. No absolute force scale or topology
uncertainty is recovered.

Halving the integration step changes GNN forecasts by at most `1.83e-6` µm and
all experimental comparisons by at most `1.35e-4` µm. Numerical refinement is
therefore far smaller than the observed predictive error. Stability alone does
not make the fitted mechanics scientifically adequate.

## Implemented scope and remaining work

Implemented: calibrated persistent 3D trajectories; reference candidate graph;
small shared GNN; bounded regional stiffness/relaxation/contraction; five explicit
constitutive families and their internal states; irreversible localized damage;
loss of load-bearing capacity; rupture-gated adhesion release; differentiable
multistep force-balanced rollout; strict future holdout; numerical/synthetic
validation; baseline comparisons; portable maps and local sensitivity.

Deferred: inferred topology, unknown damage susceptibility/localization,
edge-level constitutive mixtures, freely inferred branch ratios, learned initial
prestress, broad uncertainty ensembles, and transfer across follicles. The
current experimental performance does not justify introducing those freedoms
as if they were validated discoveries. Better constrained boundary/initial-state
assumptions and informative independent measurements are needed before making
a mechanical-network discovery claim.
